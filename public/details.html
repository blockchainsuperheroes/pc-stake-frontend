Why commit your $PC?

🏅 Your $PC keeps working

Instead of sitting idle, your committed $PC earns weekly loyalty rewards and unlocks status inside Pentagon Chain — VIP tiers, boosts on other participation rewards, and priority access to future programs.

🚫 Rewards nobody printed

Many networks pay "rewards" by minting new supply — everyone's share shrinks to pay them. Here, no new $PC is created. Rewards come from a finite, pre-funded treasury of already-earned $PC, and when it's gone, it's gone.

🔒 Your principal is untouchable

Committed $PC sits in a contract that can only return it to you, after your term. It is never lent out, never reused, never accessible to us. Code-enforced, publicly verifiable.

Be clear about what you receive

This is the most important section on this page. Read it before committing.

You receive

  • $PC on Pentagon Chain — in-ecosystem points, claimable up to once a week
  • Status & boosts tied to your commitment tier
  • Your original $PC (on Ethereum) back in full when your term ends

    • You do NOT receive

  • $PC on Ethereum — rewards are not paid on Ethereum
  • Cash, or anything redeemable for cash
  • Anything withdrawable — the bridge is one-way into Pentagon Chain; points cannot leave
    • Plain words: your reward is essentially points. $PC on Pentagon Chain is a closed-loop participation credit — it has no cash value, is non-transferable off-chain, and there is no path to convert it back to Ethereum $PC or to money. Its value is what it unlocks inside Pentagon Chain. If that's not valuable to you, don't commit. Your principal is separate: the $PC you lock stays on Ethereum — it is never bridged — and is redeemable by you in full after your term.

    How it works

    Commit $PC on Ethereum.

    Choose an amount (up to 100 $PC) and a term (3–24 months). Your $PC locks in the commitment contract.

    Your position is mirrored on Pentagon Chain.

    Two independent signers verify your lock on Ethereum and open your rewards position — the same 2-of-2 security that runs the bridge.

    Loyalty rewards accrue continuously.

    View your accrued $PC points anytime; claim them up to once per week — a single transaction entirely on Pentagon Chain, never cross-chain (how claiming works). Your rate is fixed for your whole term.

    Your $PC comes home.

    When the term ends, withdraw your original $PC on Ethereum — only your wallet can. Rewards stop accruing at term end.

    Commitment tiers — founder rates while they last

    Rates follow a published schedule, not our mood: each tranche of total committed $PC has fixed rates, and when a tranche fills, the next (lower) one opens. Whatever tranche is active when you commit is your rate for your entire term — locked on-chain, it can never be changed on you.

    When and how rates change

    We'd rather give you a schedule than a promise. Here are the exact rules:

  • Your opened position is never touched — ever. The rate you commit at is fixed on-chain for your entire term, co-signed by both program signers. No rate change, announcement, or program update can alter it.
  • New-commitment rates follow the published tranche schedule above. They step down only as tranches fill (total committed $PC crosses 10,000 → 25,000 → 50,000). The schedule is enforced on-chain — not adjusted by hand.
  • The reward pool is finite, pre-funded, and public. It fully covers the worst case of the entire schedule (every tranche filled, every position at max term). No new $PC is minted to pay rewards — when the program cap is reached, it closes.
  • Emergency discretion is one-directional. In exceptional network conditions we may lower rates for future commitments below the schedule or close new commitments early, with ≥ 7 days' notice on official channels. We can never raise a tranche's cost retroactively or reduce an opened position.
  • Rewards are solvency-limited. Claims pay only while the pool holds funds. If the pool were ever short, claims queue until it is topped up — rewards are discretionary loyalty grants, never a debt we owe you.
