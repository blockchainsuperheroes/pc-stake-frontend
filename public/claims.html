Get PC — every week

A weekly rhythm: while your $PC is locked, $PC accrue continuously, every second. Claiming sends the accrued $PC to your wallet on Pentagon Chain.

One chain, one transaction. Claiming happens entirely on Pentagon Chain — a single, small transaction. It never touches Ethereum, never uses the bridge, and nothing crosses chains. Your wallet just switches network, you confirm once, done.

The essentials

🗓 Get $PC every week

Up to once every 7 days per lock, enforced on-chain. The page shows exactly when each lock's next claim opens.

⏳ Miss a week? Nothing lost.

Rewards keep accruing whether you claim or not. Claiming weekly or monthly makes no difference to your total — unclaimed $PC simply waits for you. Accrual stops at your lock's end date; anything accrued stays claimable after that.

⛽ A tiny bit of gas — free to start

The claim transaction pays a very small gas fee in native $PC on Pentagon Chain. New to Pentagon Chain? Register at Pentagon Games and connect your wallet — registered members receive their initial gas free, so your first claim just works.

🔒 Each lock claims separately

Every lock is its own record with its own accrued $PC and its own weekly timer. Two locks = two claim buttons.

What the 3 guided steps look like

Switch to Pentagon Chain.

One click — your wallet switches (or adds) the Pentagon Chain network. Approve the switch in the wallet popup.

Confirm the claim.

A single transaction on Pentagon Chain. The page shows the exact amount before you sign.

Verified on-chain, then you're done.

The page confirms the payout landed (with a transaction link), and you can switch your wallet back to Ethereum. The claimed $PC appears in your Pentagon Chain wallet balance.

Quick answers

Can I claim while my $PC is still locked? Yes — claiming rewards and the lock on your principal are completely independent. Claim any week you like during (and after) the term.

Is claiming ever on Ethereum? No. Rewards exist only on Pentagon Chain, so claims are Pentagon Chain transactions only — cheaper and simpler, with no cross-chain step and no bridge risk.

What if the reward pool is low? Claims are paid from a finite, pre-funded pool. If it were ever short, your claim simply waits (nothing is lost) until the pool is topped up — rewards are solvency-limited by design.

Does my claimed $PC ever leave Pentagon Chain? No — it is a closed-loop, in-ecosystem credit with no cash value and no path off-chain. Their value is what they unlock inside Pentagon Chain.

Reminder: your locked principal is a separate thing entirely — it stays on Ethereum, is never bridged, and is redeemable in full after your term on the main page.
