    When and how rates change

  • Your opened position is never touched — ever. Your rate is fixed on-chain for your entire term.
  • New-commitment rates follow the published tranche schedule, stepping down only as tranches fill — enforced on-chain, not adjusted by hand.
  • The reward pool is finite, pre-funded, and public; no new $PC is minted. At the program cap, new commitments close.
  • Emergency discretion is one-directional: we may lower future rates below the schedule or close early, with ≥ 7 days' notice — never raise costs retroactively, never reduce an opened position.
  • Rewards are solvency-limited: claims pay only while the pool holds funds; if short, claims queue until top-up. Rewards are discretionary loyalty grants, never a debt.

    • Program terms (summary). The $PC Commitment Rewards Program is a discretionary loyalty program for eligible community members. Rewards are $PC on Pentagon Chain: a closed-loop, in-ecosystem participation credit with no cash value, no redemption path, and no transferability outside Pentagon Chain. Rewards are not interest, yield, dividends, or a financial return, and are not $PC on Ethereum. $PC has only ever been earned through community participation; it has never been sold by the operator, and no new $PC is minted for this Program.

    Rates. Rates for new commitments follow the published tranche schedule and are indicative loyalty rates, not interest. In exceptional network conditions the operator may lower rates for future commitments below the schedule, or close new commitments early, with advance notice consistent with prior communications; never retroactively. An opened position's rate is fixed for its term. Advertised rates are not a promise of future rates or tranche availability.

    Solvency. Rewards are payable only from the pre-funded reward pool. If the pool is exhausted, claims will not process until replenished; the operator has no obligation to replenish. Rewards are discretionary and do not constitute a debt.

    Principal. Committed $PC is time-locked in a smart contract and withdrawable only by the committing wallet after the selected term. Under current code it is not lent, invested, rehypothecated, or accessible to the operator. The Program contracts are upgradeable by the program admin — until the admin permanently and irreversibly locks upgrades on-chain (a planned maturity step); upgrades are announced in advance on official channels. Smart-contract, bridge, and blockchain risk apply; provided "as is."
