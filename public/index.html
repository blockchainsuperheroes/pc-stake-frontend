Pentagon Chain — Stake $PC

Lock $PC you already earned, for a term you choose, and receive $PC loyalty rewards on Pentagon Chain — weekly-claimable, in-ecosystem points. Your original $PC comes back to you in full after the term.

In short: rewards are essentially points — $PC on Pentagon Chain has no cash value, cannot leave Pentagon Chain, and is not $PC on Ethereum. Your locked $PC is different: it never leaves Ethereum (it is not bridged) and is redeemable by you, in full, after your term. Scroll the full terms below to enable the button.

What you receive — and don't

  • Rewards you receive: $PC on Pentagon Chain — in-ecosystem points, claimable up to once a week.
  • Rewards are NOT: $PC on Ethereum, cash, or anything withdrawable. Points cannot leave Pentagon Chain. If that's not valuable to you, don't stake.
  • Your principal is separate — and it comes back. The $PC you lock stays on Ethereum in the staking contract the whole time — it is never bridged anywhere — and it is redeemable by you, in full, after your term ends. Only the rewards live on Pentagon Chain.

    • Each stake is its own lock

    Every stake creates a separate lock with its own amount, term, rate, and unlock date — like a deposit certificate. There is no "top-up" of an existing lock and no extension; to stake more, add another lock (it gets the rates current at that moment).

    Claiming

    Points accrue continuously and are claimable up to once every 7 days per lock (on-chain throttle). Claims are a single transaction on Pentagon Chain only — they never touch Ethereum and nothing crosses chains. Unclaimed points are never lost; accrual stops at your lock's end date and anything accrued remains claimable. Claim transactions pay a small native-$PC gas fee on Pentagon Chain; registered Pentagon Games members with a connected wallet receive their initial gas free.

    Rates & the tranche schedule

    Rates for new locks follow a published tranche schedule enforced on-chain — they step down as the program fills (founder rates first, gone when their tranche fills). An opened lock's rate is fixed for its whole term and can never be changed. In exceptional conditions we may lower future rates below the schedule or close new locks early, with ≥ 7 days' notice — never retroactively. Rewards are paid from a finite, pre-funded pool of already-existing $PC (no new $PC is minted); claims are solvency-limited and pause if the pool is empty until it is topped up. Rewards are discretionary loyalty grants, not interest and not a debt.

    Eligibility

    Community members only, staking $PC they already hold — this program rewards people who earned $PC through participation. This is a loyalty program for existing holders, not an open invitation. Per-wallet limit 100 $PC. Not available in restricted jurisdictions; you are responsible for eligibility and taxes.

    Founder's SWAG package

    Members with verified VIP status and completed shipping information (via vip.pentagon.games) who lock during Tranche A qualify for a one-time Founder's merch package. Subject to availability and shipping restrictions; one per member; no cash value; not part of the reward rate.

    Contracts & risk

    Your locked $PC sits in a smart contract withdrawable only by your wallet after the term; under current code it is not lent, invested, or accessible to the operator. Contracts are upgradeable by the program admin until the admin permanently and irreversibly locks upgrades on-chain (a planned maturity step); upgrades are announced in advance. Smart-contract, bridge, and blockchain risk apply; provided "as is." This interface is open-source and community-built (source) — anyone may review, fork, or self-host it. Non-custodial: you transact directly with the contracts from your own wallet. Always verify you are on the official Pentagon Games domain.

    No advice; no offer

    Nothing here is financial, investment, legal, or tax advice, or an offer to buy any asset. Not a deposit, security, or investment; no financial return is offered or guaranteed. The operator may modify, suspend, or terminate the program prospectively, consistent with prior communications; opened locks keep their rate and their principal-return schedule. Read the $PC tokenomics.
